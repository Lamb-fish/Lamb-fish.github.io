四、公式速查

SM2 加密：

$\begin{aligned}
C_1 &= [k]G \
(x_2, y_2) &= [k]P \
t &= \mathrm{KDF}(x_2 \parallel y_2,\ \lvert M \rvert) \
C_2 &= M \oplus t \
C_3 &= \mathrm{SM3}(x_2 \parallel M \parallel y_2)
\end{aligned}$

SM2 解密：

$\begin{aligned}
(x_2, y_2) &= [d]C_1 \
t &= \mathrm{KDF}(x_2 \parallel y_2,\ \lvert C_2 \rvert) \
M &= C_2 \oplus t \
\mathrm{SM3}(x_2 \parallel M \parallel y_2) &= C_3
\end{aligned}$

SM2 密钥交换：

$\begin{aligned}
x_{1_bar} &= 2^w + (x_1 \mathbin{&} (2^w - 1)) \
x_{2_bar} &= 2^w + (x_2 \mathbin{&} (2^w - 1)) \
t_A &= (d_A + x_{1_bar}r_A) \bmod n \
t_B &= (d_B + x_{2_bar}r_B) \bmod n \
U &= [h t_A](P_B + [x_{2_bar}]R_B) \
V &= [h t_B](P_A + [x_{1_bar}]R_A) \
U &= V = [h t_A t_B]G \
&\quad \mathrm{KDF}(x_U \parallel y_U \parallel Z_A \parallel Z_B,\ 48)
\end{aligned}$

PRF-SM3：

$\begin{aligned}
A(0) &= \mathrm{seed} \
A(i) &= \operatorname{HMAC-SM3}(\mathrm{secret}, A(i-1)) \
\mathrm{P_hash} &= \operatorname{HMAC-SM3}(\mathrm{secret}, A(1)\parallel\mathrm{seed}) \parallel \cdots
\end{aligned}$

key_block 布局：

$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{CBC}: &\ \mathrm{client_MAC}(32) \parallel \mathrm{server_MAC}(32) \parallel \mathrm{client_key}(16) \parallel \mathrm{server_key}(16) \
\mathrm{GCM}: &\ \mathrm{client_key}(16) \parallel \mathrm{server_key}(16) \parallel \mathrm{client_iv}(4) \parallel \mathrm{server_iv}(4)
\end{aligned}$
